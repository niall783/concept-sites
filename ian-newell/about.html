ABOUT THE BUSINESS

A harbour workshop.
A broad engineering capability.

Boat building, refurbishment and metal fabrication from a workshop at Kilkeel Harbour. Marine experience. Practical engineering.

Rooted in the work.

Ian Newell Boat Building & Repair Ltd is a marine engineering and fabrication business based beside the slipway at Kilkeel Harbour, County Down. Its work spans new vessels and refurbishment for Irish and Scottish fishing fleets, alongside engineering parts, structural steel and bespoke manufacture.
