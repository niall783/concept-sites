How do I arrange an initial discussion?

Call the Newcastle office or use the published email address. Ask about availability, any initial consultation arrangements and what to bring.

Will my case be handled personally?

The existing practice website states that cases are handled personally by Dominic McInerney.

What should I bring?

A short timeline and copies of relevant documents are useful. Ask the office what is appropriate for your particular matter.

Does this website provide legal advice?

No. These pages introduce services. Advice and fees depend on your individual circumstances and must be agreed with the practice.
