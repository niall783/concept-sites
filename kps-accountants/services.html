GOOD TO KNOW

answers.

Which office should I contact?

KPS has offices in Downpatrick and Newcastle. Call the office most convenient for you to discuss how the team can help.

Can you help a new business?

KPS lists business start-up support among its services. Bring your plans and questions to an initial discussion.

Do you offer digital accounting support?

Yes. Bookkeeping and digital services are part of the published offering. Ask the team about the approach suitable for your business.

Where can I find current tax information?

Use the resources page for links to official GOV.UK information and the existing KPS resources. Tax rules depend on your situation and can change.
