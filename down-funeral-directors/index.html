GOOD TO KNOW

starting points.

Who should I call first?

For help with funeral arrangements, call the published Downpatrick number, 028 4461 9199. The existing website states this number is available 24 hours.

Can the service be non-religious?

The business lists non-religious ceremonies and lay funeral celebrants alongside traditional services. Discuss the wishes of your family with the team.

Where can I get a written estimate?

Contact the business directly and ask for current pricing and a written breakdown of the arrangements you are considering. No prices or plan terms are invented in this demo.
