Privacy.

How information is handled when you browse or prepare an enquiry.

Enquiry messages

The message builder works locally in your browser. It does not send or store your enquiry. Opening an email draft uses your own email app, where you can review and choose whether to send it. Please avoid including confidential details in an initial enquiry.

Telephone and external links

Telephone links open your device’s calling app. Map, website and email services have their own privacy terms.

Hosting and typefaces

This site is hosted on OpenAI Sites, which may process technical request information. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. No custom advertising trackers or analytics scripts have been added.
