Understanding your business.
Looking beyond the figures.

C Johnston and Co is a family-owned accountancy business based in Downpatrick, serving small businesses in Downpatrick, Newcastle, Ballynahinch and the wider County Down area. The firm’s published client sectors include agriculture, retail, construction, doctors, financial services, wholesalers and the motor trade. It supports sole traders, partnerships, limited companies and new businesses, with an emphasis on personal client relationships.
