ABOUT THE BUSINESS

From the first conversation.
To the last considered detail.

Kitchens, bedrooms and cabinetry designed, made and fitted by one team. Thoughtful details, made around your home.

Rooted in the work.

CNK Designs was founded by Colin Nelson, who also runs CN Joinery within the CNJ Group. Based in Ballynahinch, the team designs, manufactures and installs bespoke kitchens and bedroom furniture throughout Northern Ireland. Consultation and 3D drawings lead into in-house manufacture and installation.

GETTING STARTED

A few useful
answers.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Tell the team where you are, what you need and your intended timing. Basic dimensions, drawings or a few photographs can help explain your requirements.

How do I get a quotation?

Speak with the team to agree the scope, practical details and proposed timing. Prices and availability are confirmed directly for your requirements.

Can I discuss an individual design?

Yes. Discuss the dimensions, style and intended use with the workshop.
