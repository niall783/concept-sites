Joinery & project delivery

From the detail.
To the whole building.

Established as a partnership in 1997, J & N Joinery became incorporated in 2009. Based in Kilkeel, County Down, its portfolio covers projects across Northern Ireland, Ireland and the UK. The team’s capabilities include roofing, cladding panel installation, kitchen and shop fitting, glass and solar panel installation, general joinery, and project management. It works across commercial and public-sector construction, bespoke homes and volume residential developments.
